# My notes

This page belongs to the HTML edition of the course. In it, the ✎ button beside each section heading opens a note for that section. Notes are kept in your browser, on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Below are all your notes, page by page. Download a day's notes as `day-NN.md` to keep them with your project's `notes/` folder (the course's own convention). Download everything as one Markdown file, or back up and restore all notes as JSON, for example to move them to another browser.
